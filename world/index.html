Things you carry. Walk to the pond and you'll find a fishing rod; the lawn has a hula hoop and the playground a ball. They go into the row along the bottom — pick one with 1–8, then click to use it. Fish you catch are keepsakes; they live in your backpack.

On a tablet or phone: hold the left side to walk, drag the right side to look, and tap the ground to move there.

Around the park: 🏃 freeze tag, 🎣 fishing, 🦆 feed the ducks, 🎠 coin rides — walk up to a sign and press E. Kick the balls, wade the pond, push the rockers. Gold coins go straight into your AMG Hub balance and come back after a while.
